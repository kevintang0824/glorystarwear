Buyer control plan

Define the reference and evidence for each inspection stage

A useful sportswear QC checklist states what is being checked, which approved record controls the decision, how a result is recorded, and who can release the next stage.

Stage What to check Controlled reference Evidence to retain
Pre-production release Fit, construction, fabric, color, artwork, labels, measurements, size range, and packing route. Signed sample, current tech pack, approved size chart, artwork proof, and material codes. Approval record with revision number, date, open exceptions, owner, and release decision.
Incoming material Fabric identity, shade or print lot, width, weight, handfeel, stretch, recovery, trims, and labels. Approved coded swatch, lab dip or strike-off, material specification, and trim sample. Lot record, comparison photos, measurement results, and any segregation or corrective action.
In-line production Stitching, seam appearance, panel alignment, decoration position, workmanship, and repeat defects. Approved construction, artwork placement, workmanship standard, and first production piece. Inspection quantity, defect examples, affected operation, correction owner, and follow-up result.
Measurement and fit Points of measure, tolerance, garment balance, grading consistency, and labeled size. Approved size chart, measurement method, tolerance rules, and size-set sample. Completed measurement sheet by size, exceptions, correction, and recheck result.
Packing and final release Appearance, count, SKU, labels, barcode, polybag, assortment, carton mark, and packing list. Approved packaging artwork, SKU file, roster where relevant, carton plan, and order specification. Final report, packing photos, quantity reconciliation, exception approval, and release status.

Direct answers

Quality questions buyers ask before ordering

How does GloryStarWear control sportswear quality?

We check fabric, color, fit, measurements, stitching, logo placement, label content, packing method, carton marks, and final appearance before shipment.

Can you inspect logo and label details?

Yes. Heat transfer, embroidery, sublimation, woven labels, neck labels, hangtags, barcode labels, and polybags can be checked against approved samples.

Do you support size tolerance checks?

Yes. We compare bulk garments with the approved size chart and tolerance rules before final packing.

What should buyers approve before bulk production?

Approve fabric, color, fit sample, logo execution, label artwork, packaging, size chart, and carton packing rules before bulk cutting.

Need a clear QC plan?

Share your product category, expected order quantity, size range, and inspection requirements.
