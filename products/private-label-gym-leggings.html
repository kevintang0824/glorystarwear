Sport-specific decision map

Control gym-legging fit, coverage, recovery, pockets, and private label presentation

A leggings brief should connect the intended workout to measurable fit, coded material, physical sample evidence, branding, and SKU records rather than treating every stretch bottom as interchangeable.

01

Waistband stability and fit block

Define rise, waistband height, elastic route, waist-to-hip balance, front and back rise, gusset position, and compression target for the named fit block. Review rolling, sliding, gaping, pressure, and coverage on identified wear-test sizes before grading approval.

02

Opacity, stretch, and recovery evidence

Approve the actual fabric and color through the agreed flexion, stretch-recovery, surface, and wash checks. Terms such as squat proof, four-way stretch, compression, moisture wicking, or quick dry remain proposed claims until the test method, sample identity, acceptance rule, and result are recorded.

03

Seams, gussets, and loaded pockets

Map seams, panels, gusset edges, labels, pocket openings, phone load, hem, and decoration against squats, lunges, running, and floor contact. Record chafe, seam bulk, pocket movement, fabric distortion, and reinforcement decisions on the approved sample.

04

Color capsules, labels, and replenishment

Separate core and seasonal colors, silhouettes, fit blocks, label versions, set pairings, packaging units, barcode data, and size curves. Preserve material codes, approved samples, artwork, measurements, packing references, and substitution decisions for later replenishment.

Direct buyer answers

Private Label Gym Leggings sourcing questions, answered clearly

These short answers define what can be reviewed now, what the buyer should provide, and which terms still require project confirmation.

Can the private label gym leggings project be quoted from a reference image alone?

No. A reference image can identify a private label gym leggings direction, but a useful review also needs the requested garments, intended use, buyer market, quantity and size split, material and branding goals, packing, delivery country, and target date. Feasibility is confirmed only after the submitted project brief is reviewed.

How are fit, material, and construction routes approved?

Convert the intended movement and fit into measurable requirements, approve coded material swatches, and review the proposed construction on an identified project sample. The suggestions on this page are planning inputs, not approved specifications or universal performance claims.

How are MOQ, samples, and lead time confirmed?

MOQ, sample cost, sample rounds, and lead time are confirmed after reviewing quantity by silhouette, fit block, fabric code, color, size, pocket route, logo version, label set, packaging unit, delivery country, and launch date. No universal figure on this page is a quotation or production commitment.

What evidence should be checked before bulk release?

Bulk-release evidence: Approve an identified project sample, measurements, movement notes, waistband stability, opacity, stretch recovery, pocket function, seam comfort, color, artwork, labels, packaging, wash-test scope, and written bulk release. Branding handoff: Control logo dimensions and placement, tonal or contrast decoration, printed or woven labels, care content, hangtags, size stickers, barcode data, individual packing, set bundling, and carton marks.
