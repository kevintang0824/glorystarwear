American football range

Separate padded game, practice, and off-field requirements

American football garments use different proportions and operating rules from association football. Define the intended layer, fit group, artwork method, roster, and packing destination for each item.

Game Jerseys, Football Pants, and Practice Tops

  • Broad-sleeve contact-game jerseys, football pants, camp or practice jerseys, and coordinated warm-up apparel
  • Define garment measurements relative to the buyer's approved layering setup; protective equipment is outside this apparel scope
  • Separate padded-game, practice, youth, adult, home, and away specifications

Artwork, Numbers, and Size Groups

  • Control team marks, shoulder details, sleeve stripes, front and back numbers, nameplates, and sponsor positions
  • State sublimation, applied-mark, or mixed decoration requirements and approve appearance on the selected fabric
  • Keep youth and adult grade rules, jersey length, shoulder width, pant rise, and size-set comments separate

Roster Packs and Season Reorders

  • Pack jersey and pant sets by player, team, size, camp group, school, reseller SKU, or delivery destination
  • Connect each roster version to the approved number style, colorway, size file, label version, and carton plan
  • Maintain a replacement-piece record for late additions without changing the approved team artwork source

Buyer questions

American football uniform buyer questions

How is this page different from football or soccer kit pages?

This page covers padded-gridiron American football apparel: broad-sleeve game jerseys, football pants, practice tops, warm-ups, roster personalization, and player packing. Soccer kits remain on the separate football and soccer pages.

Does the American football uniform scope include protective equipment?

No. This page covers apparel. The buyer should provide the approved layering reference and any relevant equipment measurements; protective equipment supply, fit certification, and safety claims are outside this page's scope.

What should an American football team send for a quote?

Send the padded playing format, garment list, approved layering reference, artwork and number files, color references, youth or adult size breakdown, roster, quantity, packing rules, delivery country, and target date.
