Construction definition

Define what “seamless” means before quotation

Use a construction description, not a trend label. The proposed route must identify how the body is formed, where seams remain, which areas are knitted or cut, and what capability and supply assumptions still need confirmation.

Build the approval record

Does seamless activewear mean there are no seams?

No. Circular-knit, seam-reduced cut-and-sew, and hybrid products can retain attachment or finishing seams. The exact route and every remaining seam should be identified.

Are seamless leggings the same as yoga leggings?

No. Seamless describes construction; yoga describes use. A seamless legging still needs an activity, fit, movement, coverage, yarn, zone, size, and evidence brief.

Can body-mapped zones be customized?

A project can review zone position, knit structure, density, texture, stretch, opacity, compression target, ventilation intent, and artwork after route and feasibility confirmation.

How are seamless sizes graded?

Approve body and garment measurements, knit-program size logic, zone positions, compression and coverage across representative sizes. One base sample cannot validate the full range.

What determines seamless-activewear MOQ?

Yarn, dye or color commitment, knit program, equipment or partner availability, style and size count, trims, pads, labels, packaging, sampling, and reorder conditions all matter.

Can a reorder match the original yarn and shade?

It can be reviewed against retained records and references, but yarn, dye lot, equipment, program, finish, availability, minimums, and approved substitutions must be reconfirmed.
