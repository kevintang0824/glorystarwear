Plan common club identity, training and travel layers, sponsor versions, player data, packing, and seasonal reorder records without treating either sport's garment requirements as interchangeable.

Separate Sport-Specific Match Kits

  • Route rugby jerseys and shorts to the rugby specification; route field hockey tops, skorts, shorts, socks, and goalkeeper items to the field hockey specification
  • Approve proposed fabric, fit, movement, construction, trims, and artwork against each sport's identified sample
  • Keep names, numbers, player roles, roster sorting, team packs, and carton labels in controlled files

Training, Travel, and Warm-Up Layers

  • List training tops, warm-up jackets, track pants, hoodies, caps, and bags separately from match garments
  • Control club colors, crests, sponsors, staff versions, player allocation, and packing groups
  • Confirm each requested item's feasible manufacturing or coordinated-sourcing route in the project review

Shared Club Control System

  • Set club color references, sponsor placement, number conventions, woven or printed labels, and hangtag versions
  • Define player, team, school, or distributor packing and carton allocation
  • Record whether each item follows OEM, ODM, or coordinated sourcing only after the route is accepted in writing

What does this parent hub cover?

It covers shared club identity, training and travel layers, sponsor versions, roster fields, player or team packing, and repeat-season records for rugby and field hockey programs.

Why are there two dedicated sport pages?

Rugby contact fit and pull-zone construction differ from field hockey's low stick posture, women and youth fit blocks, skorts, liners, socks, and goalkeeper requirements.

Are all products and production routes confirmed here?

No. Exact feasibility and whether an item is manufactured or coordinated through another sourcing route are confirmed only after the submitted brief is reviewed.

What should a buyer send?

Send club colors, crest and sponsor files, sport-specific product lists, roster and sizes, quantities, packing rules, delivery country, and season date.
