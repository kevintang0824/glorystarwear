Sport-specific decision map

Build ultimate around distinct light and dark sets, readable numbers, movement, and mixed rosters

Ultimate programs need more than a full-color jersey graphic. The product record should connect light and dark differentiation, unique numbers, athletic movement, fit blocks, mixed-roster data, event packs, and later additions.

01

Light, dark, and alternate team sets

Name the event, designated home and away conventions, light shirt, dark shirt, shorts, optional alternate, base layers, and sideline pieces. Review distinction for color-vision needs and current organizer instructions; artwork on screen is not proof that opposing sets will be accepted.

02

Sprinting, throwing, and layout movement

Review shoulder, armhole, sleeve, torso length, neckline, waistband, rise, inseam, leg opening, and ground-contact zones through cuts, reaches, forehand and backhand throws, overhead movement, layouts, and recovery. Record restriction, riding, exposure, and abrasion observations by size.

03

Unique numbers and graphic legibility

Map unique player numbers, club identity, names if used, sponsor marks, front, back, sleeve and short graphics, plus solid contrast and clear zones around the number. Approve one dated light and dark artwork set and verify the event's current size and placement requirements.

04

Mixed rosters, player packs, and additions

Do not force one unisex size ratio onto every player. Record the approved fit block, garment sizes, light and dark versions, shorts, number, name, spare allocation, pack group, carton, destination, and reorder code for each roster line and late addition.

Direct buyer answers

Custom Ultimate Jerseys sourcing questions, answered clearly

These short answers define what can be reviewed now, what the buyer should provide, and which terms still require project confirmation.

Can the custom ultimate jerseys project be quoted from a reference image alone?

No. A reference image can identify a custom ultimate jerseys direction, but a useful review also needs the requested garments, intended use, buyer market, quantity and size split, material and branding goals, packing, delivery country, and target date. Feasibility is confirmed only after the submitted project brief is reviewed.

How are fit, material, and construction routes approved?

Convert the intended movement and fit into measurable requirements, approve coded material swatches, and review the proposed construction on an identified project sample. The suggestions on this page are planning inputs, not approved specifications or universal performance claims.

How are MOQ, samples, and lead time confirmed?

MOQ, sample cost, sample rounds, and lead time are confirmed after reviewing quantity by light, dark, alternate, short, warm-up, staff, or retail item, fit block, colorway, player size, name, unique number, sponsor version, spare allocation, pack group, country, and event date. No universal figure on this page is a quotation or production commitment.

What evidence should be checked before bulk release?

Bulk-release evidence: Approve identified light and dark samples, measurements, sprint and throw movement notes, color distinction and number legibility, artwork, mixed roster, player pack, weather plan, carton plan, rule review, and reorder reference. Branding handoff: Control club identity, light and dark artwork, unique player numbers, names if used, sponsor hierarchy, front, back and sleeve marks, sublimation or transfers, labels, and player, team, or event packing.
