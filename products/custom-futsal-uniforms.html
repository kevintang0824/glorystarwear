Product directions

Define the exact custom futsal uniforms range before sampling

Use this page for the indoor futsal match program: separate field-player, goalkeeper, training, warm-up, staff, and supporter products so movement, color, artwork, roster, packing, and reorder decisions stay identifiable.

Range and silhouette planning

  • Short-sleeve and sleeveless futsal jerseys, match shorts, goalkeeper tops and bottoms, training sets, warm-up jackets and pants, socks, staff apparel, and club merchandise.
  • Confirm shoulder and armhole mobility, torso and sleeve length, short rise and inseam, waistband, leg opening, goalkeeper coverage and layering, men's, women's, youth, or unisex blocks, and player allocation.
  • Buyer and use context: futsal clubs, schools, academies, leagues, tournament organizers, sports brands, distributors, and teamwear dealers.

Material and construction review

  • State target composition, weight, airflow, stretch, opacity, handfeel, snag expectation, color reference, and decoration route on coded swatches; quick-dry or durability language requires an agreed test method.
  • Review neckline, shoulder and underarm panels, side seams, hems, waistband and drawcord, reinforcement if requested, goalkeeper zones, labels, and name or number application areas.
  • Define repeated sprinting, sharp direction changes, kicking, low defensive positions, sliding or floor contact, indoor heat, goalkeeper dives, substitution frequency, and tournament wash cycles.

Branding, order, and approval control

  • Control club crest, field-player and goalkeeper colorways, names, numbers, sponsor hierarchy, sleeve and chest marks, sublimation or transfers, labels, and player or team packing.
  • Quantity by field-player, goalkeeper, training, warm-up, staff, or retail item, fit block, colorway, player size, name, number, sponsor version, spare allocation, packing group, country, and season date.
  • Approve identified field-player and goalkeeper samples, measurements, indoor movement notes, color contrast, artwork, name and number legibility, roster, player pack, carton plan, current rules review, and reorder reference.

Sport-specific decision map

Separate futsal movement, goalkeeper colors, squad data, and indoor-season reorders

Futsal should not be treated as an alternate search label for an outdoor football kit. The brief needs its own indoor movement, goalkeeper, tournament, squad, packing, and replenishment records.

01

Field-player and goalkeeper color system

Name every match, goalkeeper, alternate, training, and warm-up colorway together with the league or tournament that will review it. Confirm current contrast, number, logo, and equipment requirements with the responsible organizer; proposed artwork and samples are not proof of eligibility.

02

Indoor movement and goalkeeper coverage

Review neckline, shoulder, armhole, torso length, short rise, waistband, inseam, leg opening, and goalkeeper coverage through sprinting, cuts, shooting, low defence, floor recovery, dives, and repeated substitutions. Record restriction, riding, exposure, heat, or seam pressure by garment and player size.

03

Names, numbers, sponsors, and decoration

Create one placement map for crest, player name, number, sponsor hierarchy, sleeve marks, goalkeeper graphics, and size-dependent scaling. Approve the decoration route on the intended fabric and preserve a dated artwork version linked to the sample and roster.

04

Tournament squads, spares, and reorders

Structure the order by squad, player role, garment, fit block, size, colorway, name, number, sponsor version, and spare allocation. Preserve style codes, measurements, material and color references, artwork, pack rules, and approved substitutions for late registrations and season reorders.
