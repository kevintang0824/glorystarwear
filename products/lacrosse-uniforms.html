What to confirm for lacrosse uniforms

Lacrosse buyers usually need a clean roster file, artwork map, size list, warm-up plan, and packing rule before production.

Uniforms

Jerseys, Shorts, Pinnies

Choose game sets, practice pinnies, shooter shirts, goalie versions, and warm-up pieces.

Artwork

Names, Numbers, Sponsors

Prepare team logos, number font, sponsor marks, sleeve placements, and color references.

Fabric

Mesh and Sublimation

Select breathable mesh, durable polyester, stretch panels, and sublimation or transfer details.

Packing

Roster-Based Sorting

Pack by player, team, school, size ratio, event group, or club shop SKU.

Can GloryStarWear make lacrosse uniforms?

Yes. GloryStarWear can make lacrosse jerseys, shorts, pinnies, shooting shirts, warm-ups, socks, labels, sponsor marks, names, numbers, and team packing.

Can lacrosse uniforms be sublimated?

Yes. Sublimation works well for bold team graphics, sponsor artwork, player numbers, sleeve marks, and repeatable school or club designs.

What should a lacrosse team send?

Send mockups, logo files, roster list, size breakdown, product list, color references, quantity, packing needs, and delivery country.

Can uniforms be packed by roster?

Yes. Orders can be sorted by player, roster, school, size, team, event, or club shop SKU with labels and carton marks.
