What to decide for sports caps and bags

Accessory quality depends on fabric strength, trim behavior, logo durability, pocket layout, strap comfort, packing size, and SKU sorting rules.

Shape

Bag and Cap Structure

Confirm duffel, drawstring, tote, boot bag, equipment bag, six-panel cap, visor, or bucket hat shape.

Material

Durable Fabrics

Choose polyester, nylon, canvas, ripstop, mesh, waterproof lining, reinforced panels, and zipper quality.

Branding

Patches and Embroidery

Use embroidery, woven patches, rubber badges, heat transfer, zipper pulls, hangtags, and woven labels.

Packing

Team or Retail Sorting

Prepare SKU labels, barcode stickers, polybags, carton marks, player packs, and event bundle rules.
