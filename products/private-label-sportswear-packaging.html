Quote boundaries

Ask for the packaging minimum at component and version level

A garment order quantity does not automatically equal the practical quantity for every label, tag, sticker, bag, box, insert, or carton. Build a component table before comparing suppliers.

Stable components

Identify neutral or evergreen parts that can serve several products without carrying a size, SKU, campaign, market, date, barcode, or claim that may change.

Variable components

Map every color, size, SKU, barcode, player, language, market, campaign, channel, or destination difference to the exact label or packaging version it changes.

Physical evidence

Approve the actual garment fold, component size, artwork proof, label placement, barcode scan, closure, protection, pack appearance, carton dimensions, and count.

Commercial assumptions

Request current component MOQ, unit bundle, setup, proof and sample cost, overrun, packing labor, freight, storage, split delivery, availability, and change conditions.

FAQ

Private label sportswear packaging questions

Does packaging use the same MOQ as the garment order?

Not necessarily. Each label, tag, sticker, bag, box, insert, carton, print method, material, color, size, and version can have a different supplier minimum or unit bundle. Request a component-level answer for the current project.

Can a startup use branded packaging without fully custom boxes?

A stock or semi-custom route may combine available bags, mailers, or cartons with approved stickers, tape, inserts, sleeves, hangtags, or labels. The physical pack, branding, destination requirements, labor, and current supplier terms still need review.

Can unused custom packaging be held for future orders?

Only when ownership, quantity, condition, storage location, storage period, access, liability, version validity, reorder plan, inspection, and disposition are agreed in writing. Future use and continued availability should not be assumed.

What should be sent for a sportswear packaging quote?

Send the product and SKU map, garment fold or dimensions, component list, artwork and copy status, material and construction direction, quantity by version, packing hierarchy, barcode rules, target market and channel, destination, schedule, storage assumption, and unresolved choices.
