Product directions

Define the exact custom referee uniforms range before sampling

Use this page for referee and match-official apparel, not player uniforms, coaching apparel, certification, appointment, or governing-body authorization. Apparel does not include or certify whistles, cards, watches, timing devices, communication systems, electronic flags, headsets, body cameras, footwear, or protective equipment.

Range and silhouette planning

  • Short-sleeve and long-sleeve referee shirts, collared or crew-neck official tops, sport-permitted shorts or trousers, warm-ups, travel jackets, assistant or line-official apparel, scorer and table-official polos, and separately identified event-staff pieces.
  • Confirm collar or neckline, shoulder and armhole mobility, sleeve, torso ease and length, bottom rise and inseam, fit block, base layer, role-specific movement, and clearance around organizer-approved accessories without claiming equipment compatibility.
  • Buyer and use context: sports leagues, associations, federations, schools, universities, clubs, tournament organizers, event operators, officials groups, retailers, distributors, and teamwear dealers.

Material and construction review

  • State target composition, weight, airflow, stretch, opacity, handfeel, surface, color, and decoration route on coded swatches; cooling, wicking, visibility, durability, or weather claims require an agreed test and result.
  • Review collar, placket or zipper, shoulder and underarm panels, side seams, hems, pockets, loops, closures, labels, badge backing, and accessory-contact zones. Include pockets or attachment features only when the named sport, role, organizer, and equipment owner permit them.
  • Define the sport, official role, running or court movement, lateral reach, signaling, standing duration, indoor or outdoor climate, communication-equipment placement, pocket use, layering, and wash conditions rather than assuming one referee shirt suits every competition.

Branding, order, and approval control

  • Control official colorways, association or federation badge, competition mark, referee role, sponsor and manufacturer marks, front, back and sleeve locations, and event versions only after the buyer documents authorization to use each identity.
  • Quantity by sport, governing or organizing body, event, official role, shirt architecture, bottom, fit block, size, colorway, authorized badge and sponsor version, spare allocation, packing group, destination, and event date.
  • Approve identified apparel samples, measurements, role-specific movement notes, official-versus-player color review, pocket and accessory-clearance observations, authorized artwork record, current organizer instructions, official allocation, packing plan, written release, and reorder reference.

Sport-specific decision map

Keep officials separate from players while controlling sport rules, role identity, colors, equipment boundaries, and allocation

Referee apparel is not a generic alternate team jersey. Name the sport, governing body, competition, official role, player-uniform context, authorized marks, equipment owner, current rules, and approval authority for every version.

01

Sport, rule, event, and official-role matrix

Record the sport, governing body, competition, age or level, referee role, assistant or line role, table or scorer role, event staff boundary, current rule document, organizer instruction, and approval authority. Requirements vary by sport and event, so neither this page nor an apparel sample certifies a person, appointment, uniform, or competition eligibility.

02

Referee-player color separation and badge rights

Compare every proposed official colorway with home, away, goalkeeper, libero, alternate, warm-up, coach, and event-staff colors used in the named competition. Keep referee artwork distinct from player uniforms and obtain written permission for federation, association, league, event, sponsor, or competition marks before files are released.

03

Movement, pockets, and equipment boundary

Review running, lateral movement, signaling, standing, bending, climate, layers, collar, sleeve, torso, bottom, pockets, loops, and closures for the named role. The apparel does not include or certify whistles, cards, watches, timing or communication systems, electronic flags, headsets, body cameras, footwear, or PPE, and fit observations do not prove accessory compatibility.

04

Referee, assistant, table, and staff allocation

Separate referees, assistants, line officials, fourth officials, scorers, table officials, assessors, event staff, volunteers, player teams, and retail units. Link role, garment, size, colorway, authorized badge, sponsor version, spare quantity, person or group pack, carton, destination, and reorder code in one controlled file.
