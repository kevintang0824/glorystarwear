Direct answer

What can a custom basketball uniform manufacturer supply?

A basketball uniform manufacturer can coordinate game jerseys, matching shorts, reversible practice vests, shooting shirts, warm-up layers, socks, fan merchandise, player personalization, labels, roster packing, and repeat-order files. Buyers should approve the product route, material, fit, artwork, size and roster data, packing method, and bulk reference separately.

Decoration
