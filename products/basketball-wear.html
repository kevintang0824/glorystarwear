Direct answer

What can a custom basketball uniform manufacturer supply?

A basketball uniform manufacturer can coordinate game jerseys, matching shorts, reversible practice vests, shooting shirts, warm-up layers, socks, fan merchandise, player personalization, labels, roster packing, and repeat-order files. Buyers should approve the product route, material, fit, artwork, size and roster data, packing method, and bulk reference separately.

Recommended setup

What to customize for basketball wear

Basketball buyers usually need approved team colors, defined fabric requirements, player personalization, and clear packing by roster.

Decoration

Sublimation and Applied Marks

Select the decoration route, then define color, appearance, and wash-approval requirements for the intended fabric.

Personalization

Names and Numbers

Map player names, numbers, sponsor logos, and team marks to a controlled roster file.

Fabric

Game and Practice Direction

Define weight, construction, handfeel, ventilation goal, and approval checks for each use.

Packing

Team Sorting

Specify player packs, team sets, size groups, or retail SKUs with carton labels.

Season control

Control the basketball program across the season

Keep competition, practice, travel, fan merchandise, and reorder files separate while sharing approved club artwork.
