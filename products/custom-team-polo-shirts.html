Product directions

Define the exact custom team polo shirts range before sampling

Use this page for non-golf team, staff, coach, school, corporate, and event polo decisions. Keep golf swing, skort, resort, and pro-shop requirements on the golf page, while the parent merchandise page continues to own wider fan and retail assortments.

Range and silhouette planning

  • Short-sleeve and long-sleeve polos, coach polos, staff polos, academy polos, presentation polos, event-crew polos, corporate uniform polos, and requested private-label variants.
  • Confirm men's, women's, youth, unisex, regular, relaxed, or athletic block; shoulder and chest balance; sleeve opening; body and tuck length; hem and side vent; collar proportion; and graded size allocation.
  • Buyer and use context: sports clubs, schools, academies, coach and staff programs, corporate uniform buyers, event organizers, distributors, promotional apparel buyers, and private-label brands.

Material and construction review

  • State target composition, knit structure, weight, handfeel, stretch, airflow, opacity, color, shrinkage-review scope, and decoration route on coded swatches rather than relying on terms such as pique, interlock, dry fit, or performance alone.
  • Review self-fabric or rib collar, collar stand, placket length, button or zipper route, reinforcement, sleeve cuff, shoulder and side seams, side vents, hem, label contact, and decoration backing.
  • Define coaching, sideline, travel, hospitality, retail, office, outdoor-event, or everyday staff use; climate; layering; repeated reach; seated use; expected wash frequency; and the movement positions used for sample approval.

Branding, order, and approval control

  • Control club or company crest, staff role, sponsor marks, embroidery, transfer, print or sublimation direction, logo dimensions, placement anchors, thread or print colors, labels, hangtags, size stickers, and packing identifiers.
  • Quantity by polo style, fit block, fabric code, color, size, sleeve, collar or placket route, artwork version, staff role, label set, packing group, destination country, and required-use date.
  • Approve an identified project sample, garment measurements, movement and tuck notes, collar and placket appearance, coded material and color, decoration strike-off, label content, packing record, and written release before bulk production.

Sport-specific decision map

Separate team-polo use, collar construction, decoration, and staff allocation

A team polo should not inherit golf-specific movement or broad fan-merchandise assumptions. Record the wearer role, presentation standard, garment construction, decoration route, labeling market, and allocation method for the named project.

01

Team, staff, corporate, or event use

Identify the wearer group, indoor or outdoor setting, climate, layering, presentation level, expected wash frequency, and whether the polo is used for coaching, travel, hospitality, school, corporate uniform, event staff, resale, or promotion. These decisions should control the fit, fabric, construction, labels, and packing brief.

02

Collar, placket, cuff, and garment balance

Define self-fabric or rib collar, collar stand, placket length, button or zipper route, reinforcement, sleeve and cuff shape, shoulder, chest, body length, tuck requirement, hem, and side vents. Review collar roll, placket alignment, puckering, movement, and garment balance on the identified sample.

03

Embroidery, print, and logo placement

Map crest, company mark, staff role, sponsor, sleeve and back artwork by finished dimensions and placement anchors. Approve the proposed decoration on the intended fabric for registration, color, surface distortion, backing, edge feel, wash-review scope, and consistency across fit blocks; a digital mockup alone is not decoration evidence.

04

Staff allocation, labels, and replenishment records

Structure the order by wearer group, role, polo style, fit block, color, size, artwork version, label set, and packing unit. Preserve approved measurements, material code, decoration file, label content, individual or department allocation, carton marks, and substitution decisions for later additions or replenishment.
