MMA garment evidence

Test grappling layers and fight shorts as two separate systems

A matching set needs shared colors and artwork, but the rash guard controls close-body extension and skin-contact seams while the fight short controls stance, kicks, waistband security, closure exposure and reinforced high-load areas.

Paired-set approval

Keep matching artwork from hiding different product risks

Grappling wear trial

Use representative wearers and sizes to observe ground contact, frames, posts, torso rotation, shoulder range, compression, ride-up, seam pressure and recovery under the agreed session conditions.

Striking movement trial

Observe stance, squat, sprawl, high steps, kick range, hip rotation, side-slit opening, waistband stability, closure and drawcord behavior without exposed hard edges.

Artwork continuity

Approve shared gym colors from physical output while recording separate panel maps, print files, seam crossings, patch or transfer zones, stretch appearance and abrasion exposure.

Bulk inspection focus

Compare first output and finished lots to the identified samples for sizing, panels, seams, waistband, closure, reinforcement, artwork, labels and set allocation before release.
