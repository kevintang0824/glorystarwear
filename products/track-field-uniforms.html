Team checklist

What to confirm for track and field uniforms

Track programs need clear choices on event type, fabric weight, compression, logo placement, size ratios, and team packing.

Events

Sprint, Distance, Relay

Separate singlet, short, tight, and warm-up needs by event group and athlete type.

Fabric

Quick-Dry and Low-Chafe

Choose breathable mesh, stretch jersey, compression fabric, and seam finish for repeated race use.

Branding

School and Sponsor Marks

Prepare logos, sponsor placement, number rules, neck labels, hangtags, and color references.

Packing

Athlete or Team Sorting

Pack by athlete, size, event group, school, club, carton, or ecommerce SKU.

Can GloryStarWear make track and field uniforms?

Yes. GloryStarWear can make track singlets, running shorts, race shirts, warm-up jackets, compression layers, socks, labels, and team packing.

Can school colors and sponsor logos be added?

Yes. School logos, sponsor marks, team colors, names, numbers, heat transfers, woven labels, hangtags, and carton labels can be customized.

What should a track team send for a quote?

Send product list, event use, logo files, color references, size list, quantity, fabric target, packing needs, and delivery country.

Can track orders be packed by athlete?

Yes. Orders can be packed by athlete, event group, size ratio, school, club, or retail SKU with labels and carton marks.
