Recommended setup

What to confirm for shorts and jogger samples

Shorts and joggers need clear waistband, fabric, pocket, liner, inseam, cuff, drawcord, logo, and packaging instructions to avoid fit and cost surprises.

Fabric

Woven Stretch or Fleece

Choose lightweight woven fabric for shorts or French terry, fleece, interlock, and nylon blends for joggers.

Fit

Inseam and Block

Confirm men's, women's, unisex, 5-inch, 7-inch, 9-inch, relaxed, tapered, or athletic fit.

Trim

Waistbands and Pockets

Define elastic width, drawcord style, zipper pockets, mesh liner, side panels, reflective tape, and rib cuffs.

Retail

SKU Packing System

Use size labels, hangtags, polybags, barcode stickers, carton marks, and SKU sorting for distribution.
