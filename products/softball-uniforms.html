Product directions

Define the exact custom softball uniforms range before sampling

Use a softball-specific block and roster plan rather than copying baseball measurements: define jersey silhouette, pant or short option, rise, inseam, waist and hip balance, coverage, team graphics, and repeat-season records.

Range and silhouette planning

  • Button-front or pullover jerseys, sleeveless tops, softball pants, fitted shorts, belts, socks, visors, undershirts, jackets, coach apparel, and fan merchandise.
  • Confirm the women's, youth, or unisex fit block, bust and hip balance, jersey length, armhole, pant rise, thigh and knee ease, short inseam, waistband, hem, and player size allocation.
  • Buyer and use context: softball clubs, schools, colleges, academies, leagues, tournaments, sports brands, and teamwear distributors.

Material and construction review

  • State target jersey, pant, or short composition, weight, stretch, opacity, airflow, abrasion expectation, color, and compatible decoration on coded swatches.
  • Review placket or neckline, armhole finish, side panels, waistband, belt loops, pockets, reinforced zones if requested, hem, seam type, and name or number application areas.
  • Define throwing, batting, sprinting, sliding, crouching, base-layer use, heat, abrasion zones, coverage expectations, and repeated practice or tournament wear.

Branding, order, and approval control

  • Control team wordmark, crest, sleeve graphics, names, player numbers, embroidery or patches, visor artwork, labels, and player, team, or retail packing.
  • Quantity by jersey, pant or short, fit block, colorway, player size, name, number, visor, spare allocation, packing group, country, and season date.
  • Approve the identified jersey and bottom samples, movement and coverage notes, measurements, artwork or embroidery strike-off, roster, player pack, carton plan, and reorder reference.

Sport-specific decision map

Build softball around the selected fit block, bottom option, tournament use, and player set

Softball should not inherit baseball measurements by default. Resolve the intended women's, youth, or unisex fit architecture and approve each jersey and bottom combination through the team's actual movement and coverage needs.

01

Women, youth, or unisex fit architecture

Name the body and garment block used for the jersey and bottom, then confirm bust, waist, hip, torso, rise, thigh, armhole, sleeve, and length balance across the target sizes. Record who wore each sample and what was reviewed; one base-size fitting cannot validate every age, height, proportion, or team preference.

02

Pants, fitted shorts, and coverage

Choose softball pants, knickers, or fitted shorts by program and competition context. Define rise, inseam, leg opening, liner or base-layer use, waistband, belt loops, pockets, hem, opacity, coverage, sliding movement, and requested reinforcement. Approve the bottom with the intended jersey rather than as an isolated flat measurement sheet.

03

Sleeveless tops and tournament conditions

For sleeveless or short-sleeve jerseys, review armhole coverage, neckline, side panel, bra or base-layer interaction, underarm reach, heat, sun exposure, repeated games, wash frequency, and number placement. Specify material weight, airflow, stretch, opacity, decoration, and color on a coded swatch without turning those targets into untested performance claims.

04

Visors, player bundles, and repeat seasons

Coordinate visor style and embroidery, socks, belts, undershirts, warm-ups, player name, number, jersey size, bottom size, and spare pieces in the roster. Define individual or team packing and keep the approved fit block, measurements, artwork, colors, labels, packaging, and product codes accessible for graduating players and next-season reorders.
