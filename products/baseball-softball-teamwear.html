Product details

Baseball and softball uniform programs for schools, clubs, leagues, and fan shops

Plan team colors, jersey construction, cap and sock add-ons, sponsor marks, player names, number sets, labels, and packing rules before sampling.

Jerseys, Pants, and Team Sets

  • Button-front jerseys, raglan tops, pinstripe pants, training tees, warm-up layers, and full team sets
  • Sublimation, twill patches, embroidery, heat transfer, piping, and contrast collar details
  • Men, women, youth, school, league, tournament, and fan merchandise sizing

Caps, Belts, Socks, and Add-Ons

  • Baseball caps, belts, socks, sleeves, towels, drawstring bags, patch sets, and event merchandise
  • Club color matching, embroidered caps, woven patches, sock striping, and size labels
  • Good for league programs, school bundles, academy shops, and fan retail drops

Roster Packing and Fan Merchandise

  • Player names, numbers, sponsor panels, size sorting, club packs, barcode labels, and carton marks
  • Fan jerseys, caps, hoodies, totes, socks, patches, hangtags, and ecommerce polybags
  • Useful for repeat team orders, fan shops, event sales, and distributor programs
