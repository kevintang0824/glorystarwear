What to decide before trail apparel sampling

Trail and hiking products need clear fabric weight, packability, pocket layout, reflective placement, weather function, trim durability, and retail packing rules.

Fabric

Quick-Dry and Ripstop

Choose jersey, mesh, ripstop, stretch woven, shell, or sun-protection fabric by use case.

Function

Pockets and Packability

Plan zip pockets, cargo pockets, pouch packing, hood toggles, hem drawcords, and vest details.

Visibility

Reflective Details

Add reflective tape, heat transfers, zipper pulls, piping, and night-run safety details.

Retail

Outdoor SKU Packaging

Prepare labels, hangtags, barcode stickers, polybags, accessory packs, and carton sorting.
