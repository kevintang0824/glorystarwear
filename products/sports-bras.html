What to confirm for sports bra samples

Sports bra development needs clear support level, neckline coverage, strap stability, cup pad behavior, lining comfort, stretch recovery, and packaging presentation.

Support

Light to High Support

Match support level to yoga, gym, training, running, or lifestyle positioning.

Construction

Straps, Pads, and Underband

Confirm removable pads, cup pocket, elastic width, strap style, hook-and-eye, and longline shape.

Fabric

Nylon Spandex and Mesh

Choose smooth, ribbed, seamless, brushed, compressive, or breathable mesh fabrics.

Branding

Labels and Retail Packs

Prepare heat transfer logos, woven labels, care labels, hangtags, polybags, and carton sorting.

Fit and support validation

Turn the support target into a repeatable wear test

Test the exact size architecture, underband, front or cup shape, coverage, straps, hardware, activity, duration, wearer scope, and post-wash result. Do not treat one size label or one standing try-on as universal fit evidence.

Keep fit, odor, treatment, and health claims separate
