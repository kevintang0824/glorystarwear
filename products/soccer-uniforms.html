Approval controls

Use one roster and artwork source of truth

A crest file, player spreadsheet, and chat message should not become three conflicting records. Link the released artwork, sample, roster, pack, and change owner.

Roster

Freeze player data by version

Record player ID, name, number, top, short and sock sizes, goalkeeper status, kit version, sponsor version, approver, freeze date, and every accepted change in the teamwear roster.

Color

Approve on production-intent material

Review sublimation, transfer, embroidery or patch routes on the named fabric and garment sample. Screen color is not a textile approval; use the color-control workflow.

Youth fit

Separate age from garment measurements

Use body and garment measurements, fit blocks, movement, coverage, representative wearers, and a change process rather than assigning every player by age alone. See the youth sizing checklist.

Packing

Reconcile every player set

Check name, number, top, short, sock, goalkeeper version, spares, bag label, barcode if used, carton, destination, and exception status before release.

What is included in a soccer uniform set?

A scoped set may include match jersey, shorts, socks, goalkeeper versions, base layers, training tops, warm-ups, player names, numbers, club marks, sponsors, labels, packs, and spares.

Which page should a US soccer club use?

Use this page for a US soccer season built around home, away, goalkeeper, player roster, youth and adult sizes, team packing, late additions, and reorders.

How are names, numbers, and sponsors controlled?

Keep one dated roster and artwork source with player ID, garment sizes, name, number, sponsor version, style, colorway, approver, freeze date, and change log.

Can late player additions be reordered?

A reorder can be reviewed against retained style, pattern, material, color, artwork, roster, label, and packaging records. Availability, minimums, timing, and substitutions must be reconfirmed.

Does GloryStarWear certify league compliance?

No. The buyer or event organizer must identify and confirm the current rules. GloryStarWear can use those inputs to review artwork, colors, samples, roster, and requested evidence.

What is required for a soccer-uniform quote?

Send the program list, competition, fit blocks, roster and spares, artwork and colors, material target, decoration route, quantities, packing groups, destination, and target date.
