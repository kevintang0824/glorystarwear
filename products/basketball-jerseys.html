Can GloryStarWear make custom basketball jerseys?

Yes. GloryStarWear can develop game and reversible basketball jerseys with controlled fabric, fit, neckline, armhole, artwork, names, numbers, sponsor marks, labels, and player packing. Use the basketball uniform program page when shorts, warm-ups, or fan merchandise are also required.

Can jerseys be packed by player roster?

Yes. Basketball jerseys can be packed by player, size set, team, retail SKU, barcode label, and carton marking requirements.

What should I send for a basketball jersey quote?

Send reference images, team colors, logo files, roster list, size range, quantity, fabric direction, customization method, packing rules, delivery country, and timeline.
