Product directions

Define the exact custom darts shirts range before sampling

Use this page for darts-specific collar and shirt architecture, repeated throwing-arm movement, stage dress, player identity, sponsor zones, event packs, retail versions, and replenishment records.

Range and silhouette planning

  • Collared darts shirts, polos, zip-neck jerseys, crew-neck practice tops, women's and men's cuts, youth shirts, warm-up jackets, staff apparel, and supporter merchandise.
  • Confirm collar or neckline, shoulder and armhole mobility, sleeve opening, chest and torso ease, front and back length, side split, women's, men's, youth, or unisex blocks, and player size allocation.
  • Buyer and use context: darts players, clubs, teams, tournament organizers, sports brands, retailers, fan-merchandise sellers, and teamwear distributors.

Material and construction review

  • State target composition, weight, airflow, stretch, handfeel, surface, opacity, color and decoration route on coded swatches; cooling, wicking, or durability claims require an agreed test and result.
  • Review collar, placket or zipper, shoulder and underarm panels, sleeve finish, side seams, body hem, labels, sponsor zones, and raised or stiff decoration through the complete throwing motion.
  • Define throwing stance, shoulder and elbow movement, repeated arm extension, torso stability, standing duration, stage heat and lighting, travel use, practice versus competition wear, and wash frequency.

Branding, order, and approval control

  • Control player name, club or country identity, sponsor hierarchy, tournament-supplied marks, front, back and sleeve graphics, sublimation, transfers, embroidery, labels, and player, event, or retail packing.
  • Quantity by shirt architecture, fit block, colorway, player size, name, club or country identity, sponsor version, event mark, spare allocation, packaging unit, destination, and competition date.
  • Approve an identified shirt sample, measurements, throwing-arm movement notes, collar and placket review, artwork and sponsor positions, player list, dress-code check, pack plan, retail version, and reorder reference.

Sport-specific decision map

Control darts throwing movement, stage dress, sponsor hierarchy, and player versions

Darts shirts need a product and artwork record distinct from bowling or generic esports jerseys. Confirm the competition, organizer, stage dress code, supplied sponsor marks, and approval deadline for every event version.

01

Collared competition or practice shirt

Choose the collar, placket or zip route, sleeve shape, fit block, body length, side split, practice version, and warm-up pieces before artwork. State whether the order is for league play, floor matches, stage matches, player retail, or supporter merchandise because dress expectations can differ.

02

Throwing shoulder and repeated stance

Review shoulder, armhole, sleeve opening, upper back, chest, torso length, hem, collar, and decoration through the stance, draw, release, follow-through, and repeated throwing. Record restriction, twisting, riding, heat, glare, or seam pressure on the identified size sample.

03

Player, club, tournament, and sponsor map

Map player name, club or country identity, personal sponsors, event sponsors, organizer-supplied stickers or marks, front, back and sleeve locations, and size scaling. Verify the current event dress and advertising instructions; artwork approval is not universal competition clearance.

04

Event versions, retail units, and reorders

Separate player competition shirts, alternates, practice tops, staff pieces, supporter versions, signed merchandise, retail sizes, and replacements. Preserve shirt code, fit block, size, artwork, sponsor version, labels, packaging, destination, and approved substitutions for each release.
