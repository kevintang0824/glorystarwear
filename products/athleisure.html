Development routes

Choose a light-custom, ODM, or OEM route before setting expectations

The route changes the inputs, sample depth, decision ownership, and commercial variables. No route has one universal MOQ or lead time.

Light custom

Adapt an existing direction

Confirm the exact available base, permitted fabric or color changes, decoration, labels, packaging, size range, availability date, and what cannot change.

ODM

Develop from references

Translate references into measurable fit, fabric, construction, trim, artwork, label, packaging, and evidence requirements; references do not become approved specifications by themselves.

OEM

Build from a controlled tech pack

Review pattern and measurement ownership, bill of materials, graded sizes, construction, tolerances, artwork, test requirements, packaging, and change-control responsibilities.

Reorder

Match the retained record

Check approved samples, patterns, material and trim availability, color lots, artwork, labels, packaging, quantities, destinations, and accepted substitutions before confirming repeatability.

Direct buyer answers

Athleisure manufacturing questions, answered with boundaries

What products belong in an athleisure collection?

Common routes include hoodies, sweatshirts, joggers, tracksuits, oversized tees, tanks, shorts, light jackets, lifestyle sets, labels, and retail packaging. The right mix depends on the target customer and SKU plan.

What is the difference between athleisure and training wear?

Athleisure prioritizes lifestyle fit, retail presentation, coordinated drops, and repeatable brand details. Training wear starts from sport movement, sweat, stretch, support, and performance evidence.

Can hoodies and joggers use the same fabric and color?

They can be planned as a set, but weight, shrinkage, handfeel, face and back, rib, lot, dye route, and garment construction still need physical approval across both pieces.

What affects MOQ for an athleisure drop?

Style count, fabric and color commitment, trims, washes, decoration, labels, packaging, size range, development route, supplier availability, and split shipments all affect the confirmed boundary.

What should an athleisure quote brief include?

Send the product and SKU list, references or tech packs, target fit, fabric and weight, colors, decoration, labels, packaging, quantities, size curve, destination, and launch date.

How are samples and reorders controlled?

Retain approved measurements, materials, trims, colors, artwork, labels, packaging, sample identity, decisions, and substitution rules. A future reorder is reconfirmed against availability and current conditions.
