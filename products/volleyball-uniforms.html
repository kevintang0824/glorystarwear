Eight sample checks

Validate the garment before attaching the full roster

  1. 1

    Identify the product and reference set

    Record style, colorway, role, size, sample ID, tech pack, pattern or measurement revision, material codes, artwork, ruleset decision, label files, roster status, and every open substitution or exception.

  2. 2

    Measure the jersey consistently

    Define points of measure, method, condition, values, grade rules, and tolerances for chest, body length, shoulder, sleeve, neckline, armhole, hem, and artwork anchors that control the intended fit and appearance.

  3. 3

    Review volleyball movement and coverage

    Use named players or fit participants and a recorded sequence such as ready position, overhead reach, serve, set, attack approach, jump, block, lateral movement, dig, and floor transition where appropriate. Record garment movement, restriction, exposure, seam contact, and adjustment rather than claiming universal comfort.

  4. 4

    Test each bottom route separately

    Do not apply one short result to tights, leggings, loose shorts, other inseams, or another fit block. Record waistband stability, rise, gusset, liner, hem, thigh contact, ride-up, shift, opacity, knee-pad interaction, movement, recovery, and representative wearer scope.

  5. 5

    Measure production artwork on garments

    Check number, name, crest, sponsor, manufacturer, patch, and other marks against the released source, method, finished dimensions, position anchors, garment seams, panels, sizes, colors, registration, and physical sample.

  6. 6

    Compare primary, alternate, and libero sets

    Keep every front and back revision identifiable. Confirm club identity, garment and bottom compatibility, number and roster data, and the authorized ruleset decision without treating the garment developer as the competition approver.

  7. 7

    Wash and remeasure the approved route

    Use the intended care procedure and named number of cycles. Record dimensional change, twist, puckering, color or print observations, decoration condition, elastic recovery, labels, and any change to fit or artwork position. One result does not prove every production lot.

  8. 8

    Build and verify a packed player set

    Reconcile coded player, role, unique number, jersey size, bottom size, primary, alternate, libero status, labels, quantity, bag, pack mark, carton, destination, spare, and replacement record before bulk release.
