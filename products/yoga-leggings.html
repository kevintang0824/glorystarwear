Clear product boundaries

Yoga, gym, and seamless leggings require different briefs

This page focuses on yoga and studio mobility, soft or controlled handfeel, waistband behavior, gusset, seams, and retail fit. Use private label gym leggings for lifting support, higher-compression routes, pockets, abrasion, and gym wear tests. Use seamless activewear for yarn, knit program, body-mapped zones, and seam-reduced construction. A no-front-seam cut-and-sew legging is not automatically seamless.

Build the approval record

Can a yoga-legging project be customized?

High-waist, V-waist, flare, pocket, scrunch, and biker-short directions can be reviewed with project-specific fabric, fit, artwork, labels, packaging, quantity, and feasibility confirmation.

Are yoga leggings and gym leggings the same?

Not necessarily. Yoga briefs prioritize studio mobility, seam comfort and waistband behavior; gym leggings prioritize lifting support, compression, pockets, coverage, and abrasion conditions.

Does no-front-seam mean seamless?

No. A cut-and-sew legging can omit the front rise seam; seamless describes a knitting or construction route that must be confirmed separately.

How should squat-proof opacity be approved?

Review the actual style, fabric, color, representative sizes, lighting, movement sequence, care state, and agreed acceptance method. One swatch or standing photo is not enough.

What affects yoga-legging MOQ and reorders?

Style, fabric and color commitment, size range, decoration, labels, packaging, retained material availability, and approved substitution rules all affect the confirmed boundary.

What quote details are needed?

Send style references, intended movement, fabric and handfeel target, size range, colorways, waistband and gusset route, artwork, quantity, packaging, country, and target date.
