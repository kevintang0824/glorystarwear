Fit planning

What matters for plus size activewear samples

Inclusive activewear needs more than a larger pattern. Buyers should confirm fit intent, grading, support, recovery, opacity, and comfort across the full size range.

Size Range

Grading and Fit Blocks

Define the size range, base size, grading increments, intended body measurements, and fit sample plan.

Waistbands and Sports Bras

Confirm waistband height, anti-roll construction, underband control, strap width, and support level.

Opacity and Recovery

Check squat-proof opacity, stretch recovery, pilling, colorfastness, handfeel, and seam durability.

Labels and Packaging

Plan size labels, hangtags, barcode stickers, polybags, size ratio cartons, and ecommerce SKU sorting.

Fit-range evidence

A wider size range needs representative physical validation

Plan core sizes and any short, regular, tall, curvy, fuller-bust, or other proportion routes as named pattern and sample decisions. Review waist-to-hip shape, rise, knee and ankle position, compression, movement, wash, and range transitions before treating a size label as approved.

Can GloryStarWear manufacture plus size activewear?

Yes. GloryStarWear can develop plus size leggings, sports bras, gym tops, shorts, joggers, hoodies, matching sets, labels, and packaging for private label activewear buyers.

Yes. Buyers can provide target measurements, size range, base size, fit notes, product references, and market requirements before sample development.
