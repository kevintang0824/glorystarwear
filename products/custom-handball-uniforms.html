  • Short-sleeve and sleeveless handball jerseys, match shorts, goalkeeper jerseys and pants, training tops and shorts, warm-up jackets and pants, staff apparel, socks, and club merchandise.

Sport-specific decision map

Control handball throwing movement, goalkeeper contrast, artwork, and season reorders

A handball program needs distinct field-player and goalkeeper records, current competition-rule verification, high-movement sample checks, and a roster that survives additions and repeat seasons.

01

Field-player and goalkeeper color system

Name every field-player, goalkeeper, alternate, warm-up, and staff colorway and the competition or league that will review it. Confirm current contrast, number, logo, and equipment rules with the responsible organizer; proposed artwork is not proof of eligibility.

02

Overhead throwing and contact movement

Review neckline, shoulder, sleeve, armhole, underarm panel, torso length, short rise, waistband, inseam, and leg opening through throwing, blocking, jumping, landing, sprinting, contact, and floor recovery. Record restriction, riding, exposure, or seam pressure by sample and player size.

03

Names, numbers, sponsors, and size scaling

Create one placement map for crest, player number, name, sponsor hierarchy, sleeve marks, goalkeeper graphics, and size-dependent scaling. Approve the decoration method on the intended fabric and preserve a dated artwork version linked to the roster and sample.

04

Player packs, spares, and repeat seasons

Structure the order by squad, player, role, garment, fit block, size, colorway, name, number, sponsor version, and spare allocation. Preserve approved style codes, measurements, materials, colors, artwork, pack rules, and substitutions for late additions and reorders.

Direct buyer answers

Custom Handball Uniforms sourcing questions, answered clearly

These short answers define what can be reviewed now, what the buyer should provide, and which terms still require project confirmation.

Can the custom handball uniforms project be quoted from a reference image alone?

No. A reference image can identify a custom handball uniforms direction, but a useful review also needs the requested garments, intended use, buyer market, quantity and size split, material and branding goals, packing, delivery country, and target date. Feasibility is confirmed only after the submitted project brief is reviewed.

How are fit, material, and construction routes approved?

Convert the intended movement and fit into measurable requirements, approve coded material swatches, and review the proposed construction on an identified project sample. The suggestions on this page are planning inputs, not approved specifications or universal performance claims.

How are MOQ, samples, and lead time confirmed?

MOQ, sample cost, sample rounds, and lead time are confirmed after reviewing quantity by field-player, goalkeeper, training, warm-up, staff, or retail item, fit block, colorway, player size, name, number, sponsor version, spare allocation, packing group, country, and season date. No universal figure on this page is a quotation or production commitment.

What evidence should be checked before bulk release?

Bulk-release evidence: Approve identified field-player and goalkeeper samples, measurements, throwing and court-movement notes, color contrast, artwork, name and number legibility, roster, player pack, carton plan, rule review, and reorder reference. Branding handoff: Control club crest, player names and numbers, goalkeeper and field-player colorways, sponsor hierarchy, sleeve and chest marks, sublimation, transfers, embroidery, labels, and player or team packing.
