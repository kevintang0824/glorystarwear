Sport-specific decision map

Build padel apparel around overhead movement, ball storage, climate, and club-retail operations

Padel collections combine court performance, social-club presentation, event apparel, and retail replenishment. Each use needs an identifiable garment, sample, artwork, packing, and reorder record.

01

Overhead reach and rapid court movement

Review shoulder, armhole, neckline, torso length, waistband, rise, inseam, liner, skirt or dress coverage, and hem through smashes, volleys, low defence, lateral steps, turns, and sprints. Approve the named fit block on physical samples rather than a standing image.

02

Ball pockets, liners, and storage routes

Define whether the garment uses side pockets, dedicated ball storage, liner pockets, or no storage. Test opening access, retention, load movement, fabric distortion, seam pressure, coverage, and left-right placement during the intended court movements.

03

Indoor, outdoor, and performance claims

Record venue, climate, season, heat, sun exposure, sweat, wash frequency, material weight, airflow, stretch, opacity, and surface needs. UV protection, cooling, quick dry, moisture wicking, or durability remain proposed claims until test methods and results are agreed.

04

Club identity, events, and retail replenishment

Separate academy uniforms, member apparel, coach kit, tournament items, sponsor versions, resort retail, ecommerce SKUs, and core replenishment colors. Preserve sizes, artwork, labels, barcodes, packaging, allocation, approved references, and substitutions by style.

Direct buyer answers

Custom Padel Apparel sourcing questions, answered clearly

These short answers define what can be reviewed now, what the buyer should provide, and which terms still require project confirmation.

Can the custom padel apparel project be quoted from a reference image alone?

No. A reference image can identify a custom padel apparel direction, but a useful review also needs the requested garments, intended use, buyer market, quantity and size split, material and branding goals, packing, delivery country, and target date. Feasibility is confirmed only after the submitted project brief is reviewed.

How are fit, material, and construction routes approved?

Convert the intended movement and fit into measurable requirements, approve coded material swatches, and review the proposed construction on an identified project sample. The suggestions on this page are planning inputs, not approved specifications or universal performance claims.

How are MOQ, samples, and lead time confirmed?

MOQ, sample cost, sample rounds, and lead time are confirmed after reviewing quantity by match, training, coaching, social, event, or retail style, fit block, color, size, pocket route, artwork version, packaging unit, club or store allocation, country, and launch or event date. No universal figure on this page is a quotation or production commitment.

What evidence should be checked before bulk release?

Bulk-release evidence: Approve an identified project sample, measurements, overhead and lateral movement notes, pocket or ball-retention check, liner and coverage observations, fabric and color, artwork, labels, packaging, allocation plan, and written bulk release. Branding handoff: Control club crest, sponsor hierarchy, event marks, player or staff names, color capsules, transfers, embroidery, labels, hangtags, barcode data, retail packs, club allocation, and carton marks.
