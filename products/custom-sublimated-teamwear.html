Artwork and packing

What a sublimated teamwear quote needs

Teamwear costs and lead times depend on artwork readiness, fabric choice, roster details, number rules, add-ons, packing method, and size sorting.

Artwork

Vector Logos and Sponsor Files

Send AI, PDF, EPS, SVG, or high-resolution logo files with crest, sponsor, name, and number placement notes.

Roster

Names, Numbers, and Sizes

Prepare a roster spreadsheet with player names, jersey numbers, sizes, gender or age group, and team group.

Use the roster packing guide
Fabric

Mesh, Interlock, or Stretch

Choose breathable polyester mesh, interlock jersey, stretch fabric, or sport-specific panels by use case.

Packing

Player Packs and Carton Marks

Decide whether each player gets a pack with jersey, shorts, socks, labels, and team carton sorting.

Color approval gate

Approve color on the intended fabric and production route

A club color code or screen mockup is an input, not proof of the finished jersey. Before bulk release, connect the current artwork revision to the named target, output profile and print setup, ink or dye system, transfer medium, actual fabric code and base color, process conditions, physical strike-off or garment sample, comparison method, tolerance where applicable, approver, and retained evidence.

Use the sportswear sublimation color matching guide and free 47-field register to control that decision. The guide does not promise an exact match or replace project-specific color-management, measurement, customer, or brand approval requirements.

Can GloryStarWear make sublimated teamwear?

Yes. GloryStarWear can produce sublimated basketball jerseys, football kits, volleyball uniforms, baseball teamwear, shorts, socks, sponsor marks, names, numbers, labels, and roster packing.

What is needed for sublimated jersey artwork?

Send vector logo files, sponsor artwork, club colors, name and number rules, product references, size range, quantity, placement notes, and the target and physical approval requirements for critical colors.

Can team orders be packed by player?

Yes. Teamwear orders can be sorted by player, size, number, team, colorway, school, league, or carton mark for easier distribution.

Who is this page best for?
