Netball uniform range

Choose the garment route before artwork and position planning

A-line dresses, bodysuits, tops with skorts or shorts, training pieces, and warm-ups need different fit and coverage decisions. Record the uniform route before applying a shared club identity.

Dresses, Bodysuits, Tops, and Skorts

  • A-line and fitted dresses, bodysuits, team tops, skorts, shorts, training singlets, and warm-up layers
  • Define neckline, armhole, length, panel map, inner-short or lining direction, stretch, coverage, and movement approval
  • Keep women, youth, match, training, home, away, and club-shop specifications distinct

Club Artwork and Position System

  • Control team colors, crest, sponsor positions, side panels, front and back artwork, and approved color references
  • Define removable bib or position-panel dimensions, attachment method, contrast, set count, and packing rule
  • Use the same approved artwork source while keeping garment-specific panel maps and fit files separate

Team Packs, Size Groups, and Reorders

  • Pack by player, position set, team, school, size group, association allocation, reseller SKU, or event
  • Connect dress or set style code, approved sample, size file, position system, label version, and carton rule
  • Plan junior additions, replacement garments, and new position sets without changing unrelated approved pieces

Buyer questions

Netball uniform buyer questions

What garments can be included in a netball uniform program?

A netball program can include dresses, bodysuits, tops, skorts, shorts, training singlets, warm-up jackets, socks, position panels or bib sets, labels, and team packing. Confirm the final garment architecture before sampling.

What should be defined for netball position panels or bibs?

Define the panel or bib dimensions, attachment method, contrast, position grouping, number of sets, labeling, storage, and whether each set is packed by player, team, or club.

What should a netball club send for a quote?

Send the garment route, reference designs, crest and sponsor files, position system, women's and youth size breakdown, quantity, roster or team allocation, warm-up needs, packing plan, delivery country, and target date.

Prepare a netball uniform brief

Send the dress, bodysuit or set route, position system, artwork, size breakdown, quantity, team allocation, warm-up list, packing rule, and delivery target.
