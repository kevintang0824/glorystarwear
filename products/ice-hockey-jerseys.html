Ice hockey apparel range

Separate jersey cut, artwork route, sock system, and roster

Ice hockey jerseys layer differently from field hockey tops and ordinary team shirts. Define the buyer's approved layering reference, skater or goalie cut, sleeve proportion, decoration route, matching socks, and player pack.

Skater, Goalie, and Practice Jerseys

  • Wide-cut skater jerseys, separate goalie-cut jerseys, lightweight practice versions, and supporter variants
  • Define garment measurements relative to the buyer's approved layering setup; protective equipment is outside this apparel scope
  • Keep home, away, alternate, practice, youth, adult, skater, goalie, and supporter specifications distinct

Crests, Numbers, Nameplates, and Socks

  • Control crest shape, shoulder marks, sleeve stripes, front and back numbers, nameplate dimensions, and sponsor zones
  • Confirm sublimation, applied-mark, or mixed decoration requirements and approve appearance and handfeel on the chosen fabric
  • Map jersey colorways to matching hockey socks, warm-ups, fan pieces, and club-shop variants

Player Packs and Replacement Jerseys

  • Pack jerseys and socks by player, team, size, camp group, school, reseller SKU, or delivery destination
  • Connect each roster version to the approved cut, number style, colorway, sock set, label version, and carton plan
  • Maintain a replacement-piece record for late additions without changing the approved artwork source

Buyer questions

Ice hockey jersey buyer questions

How is this page different from field hockey teamwear?

This page covers ice hockey apparel: skater, goalie and practice jersey cuts, matching hockey socks, warm-ups, roster personalization, and player packing. Field hockey remains on the separate rugby and field hockey page.

Does the ice hockey jersey scope include protective equipment?

No. This page covers apparel. The buyer should provide the approved layering reference and relevant equipment measurements; protective equipment supply, fit certification, and safety claims are outside this page's scope.

What should an ice hockey team send for a quote?

Send the jersey cut list, approved layering reference, home, away and practice artwork, crest and number files, sock requirements, youth or adult size breakdown, roster, quantity, packing rules, delivery country, and target date.

Prepare an ice hockey jersey brief
