Cricket uniform range

Build the kit around format, garment role, and club operations

Test, one-day, T20, training, travel, and retail programs need different garment maps. Define which pieces share colors and artwork without treating every format as the same set.

Whites, Colored Kits, and Training Pieces

  • Traditional white shirts and trousers, colored short- or long-sleeve jerseys, training vests, and practice tops
  • Define collar, placket, sleeve, trouser rise, waistband, pocket, sweater, vest, and cap requirements by format
  • Record which garments are match, training, travel, supporter, or retail SKUs before requesting samples

Club Artwork and Size System

  • Control club crest, sponsor positions, player name or number rules, color references, and artwork versions
  • Separate adult and youth fit groups, shirt length, trouser measurements, sweater fit, and size-set approval
  • Use one approved source file for each white, colored, training, travel, and off-field variation

Player Packs and Reorders

  • Choose player packs, team sets, size groups, academy allocations, retail SKUs, or distributor carton plans
  • Keep style code, approved sample, roster version, cap option, label version, and carton rule together
  • Plan late player additions and replacement garments without reopening unrelated artwork

Buyer questions

Cricket uniform buyer questions

What can be included in a custom cricket uniform program?

A cricket program can include white or colored shirts, trousers, training vests, sweaters, sleeveless knit layers, caps, socks, warm-up apparel, labels, and player packing. Confirm the exact garment list before sampling.

Should Test whites and T20 kits use the same specification?

Not automatically. Record the color system, shirt construction, artwork, trouser details, sweater or vest needs, size files, and approval sample for each format that differs.

What should a cricket club send for a quote?

Send the format and garment list, reference designs, crest and sponsor files, color references, quantity and size breakdown, player data, cap or warm-up needs, packing plan, delivery country, and target date.

Prepare a cricket uniform brief

Send the match formats, garment matrix, crest and sponsor files, size breakdown, quantity, player data, cap or warm-up list, packing rule, and delivery target.
