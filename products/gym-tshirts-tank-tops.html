Performance evidence

Do not approve “quick-dry” from a fabric name alone

Define whether the brief needs liquid transport, wicking, drying, sweat visibility, wet cling, breathability, or a garment wear comparison. Use the activewear moisture-management test guide and free register to connect the claim to the exact fabric, color, garment zone, care state, conditions, criteria, evidence, and bulk scope.

Odor and antibacterial evidence

Separate wash-return odor from an antibacterial claim

A treatment name or antibacterial swatch result does not automatically prove lower wearer odor, freshness after care, or protection from germs. Use the activewear odor-resistance and antibacterial test guide to map residual odor, odor rebloom, adsorbency, bacterial-odor reduction, care state, controls, market review, and released wording.
