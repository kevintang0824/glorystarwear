Direct answer

How should a buyer verify factory photos or video?

Treat each photo, clip, file set, or live walkthrough as dated and scoped evidence. Confirm the supplier and site named, capture date, requested process, product or order reference, continuity or editing status, file metadata where available, confidentiality, reviewer, and limitations. Media verifies only the scene, time, and scope captured; it does not by itself prove legal identity, site ownership, continuous capacity, certificate scope, quality repeatability, or whole-order conformity.

Identity and location

Name the supplier, contracting entity, facility, claimed location, facility role, participants, and visible identity or location cues. Do not infer ownership from a sign, room, or single clip.

Date and continuity

Record whether the media is live or recorded, its actual capture date and time zone, original filename or call reference, and whether the sequence is continuous, edited, or unknown.

Process and product scope

Ask for the exact checkpoint, scene, action, product, style, sample, order, stage, lot, and approved reference needed for the decision. Record what was not shown.

Authorization and limits

Confirm confidentiality, redaction, publication permission, evidence location, reviewer, open issues, and next action. Protect customer logos, workers, personal data, and confidential production.

Buyer FAQ

Factory media questions

Does this page contain verified GloryStarWear factory footage?

No. The four current visuals are illustrative planning images, and this page does not currently publish source-verified GloryStarWear factory photographs or video. Use the register to scope and assess any project-specific media requested or received.

Can buyers request a factory video call?

Buyers may ask whether one is available, but this page does not confirm or guarantee a live call. Availability, the participating entity and site, processes, scheduling, access, confidentiality, and recording permission must be confirmed separately for the current project.

What should a factory video include?

When a shortlisted supplier confirms video is available, ask it to show the named facility and current date cues, the requested process, identified product or order references, a clear sequence, and the exact checkpoint needed for the decision. Record what was not shown.

Can product-specific photos be shared?

Ask the supplier to confirm that relevant photos exist, where and when they were captured, what product or order they show, whether the source can be verified, and whether customer, worker, facility, or confidentiality restrictions allow sharing.

Build a scoped factory media request

Record the project, named site, exact checkpoint, product or order reference, media format, confidentiality needs, reviewer, and decision gate. Any photo, clip, file set, or live-call availability must be confirmed separately and is not promised by this form.
