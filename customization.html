Decoration decision

Choose a sportswear printing method that fits the garment

The right method depends on fabric composition, stretch, artwork coverage, surface texture, wash expectations, order structure, and the sample evidence available for approval.

Method Common use Buyer approval points Planning note
Sublimation Polyester teamwear, all-over graphics, sponsor marks, names, and numbers. Panel artwork, color target, seam alignment, name and number data, and printed sample. Best planned with the actual pattern because artwork position changes across panels and sizes.
Heat transfer Activewear logos, player details, small graphics, and multicolor marks. Film type, size, position, edge quality, handfeel, stretch behavior, and wash reference. Test the selected transfer on the approved production fabric rather than on a substitute swatch.
Screen print Training tees, hoodies, sweats, and repeat flat-color artwork. Ink color, opacity, print size, placement, curing, surface feel, and appearance after care testing. Artwork color count and fabric surface affect the setup and the achievable finish.
Embroidery Polos, jackets, caps, bags, badges, and premium brand marks. Thread color, stitch density, backing, edge definition, puckering, size, and placement. Dense artwork may need simplification to reproduce cleanly on lightweight or stretch fabric.
Woven, silicone, or rubber badge Brand trims, team crests, sleeve marks, zipper pulls, and packaging details. Dimensions, thickness, edge, color, attachment method, placement, and approved physical sample. The badge and attachment route can create separate component minimums and approval timing.

Direct answers

Custom sportswear printing questions

What printing method is best for custom sportswear?

The best method depends on the garment fabric, stretch, artwork coverage, surface texture, wash expectation, and required finish. Sublimation commonly fits polyester teamwear, while heat transfer, screen print, embroidery, and badges suit different fabrics and logo types.

Is sublimation suitable for every sportswear fabric?

No. Sublimation is normally planned for compatible polyester fabrics. Fabric construction, color, handfeel, print result, and the actual garment panel artwork should be approved before bulk production.

What should buyers approve before bulk logo production?

Approve the artwork file, color target, dimensions, placement, production fabric, decoration sample, edge and surface quality, stretch behavior where relevant, label content, and the controlled sample reference.

Can labels and packaging follow the same brand system?

Yes. Neck labels, care labels, woven labels, hangtags, barcode stickers, polybags, boxes, inserts, and carton marks can be planned together, with each artwork file and placement approved separately.
