## Research signal

Recent Reddit discussions repeatedly separated these problems:

- waist fit versus hip, seat, or thigh fit;
- garment length versus correct knee, calf, ankle, pocket, and design-line position;
- rise and waistband position versus torso proportion;
- alpha-size changes versus product-specific proportion routes;
- one standing fit result versus movement, pocket load, care, and fabric recovery.

Reddit was used for question discovery and customer language only.
