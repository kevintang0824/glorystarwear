## 使用规则

这份内容可以帮助团队快速整理页面，但不是事实来源。发布前必须完成：

1. 每个数字、主体、地点、日期、周期、数量、结果和证书信息都能在原始资料中定位。
2. 图片、视频、客户名称、Logo、评价和订单资料都有公开授权或已完成脱敏。
3. 证书、报告和核验链接对应实际持证主体、工厂、产品、材料或订单范围。
4. 页面只描述资料能证明的范围，不把供应商、材料商或合作工厂的资质写成 GloryStarWear 自有资质。
5. 完成替换后，删除本文件中的 `[[待核验]]` 标记，再更新 `dateModified` 和页面结构化数据。

## 5. FAQ 页面：`faq.html`

这些问题可以在证据资料完成后加入 FAQ；在没有资料时，继续使用当前“待验证”披露，不要填入推测数字。

### Can buyers review factory evidence?

`Yes, where public disclosure is authorized. We can identify the documented entity relationship, location at city or region level, dated production media, verified process scope, and any limitations. Evidence is date- and project-specific, so buyers should request the current records for the product and order being discussed.`

### Do you publish certificates?

`We publish a certificate or test document only after checking its holder, issuer, number, dates, scope, and verification route. A document may cover a material, product, site, lot, or transaction rather than the whole business. The page states those limits instead of treating one document as universal proof.`

### Can you show completed customer projects?

`We publish project information only with customer approval or after appropriate anonymization. Each public case identifies the product scope, development records, quality checkpoint, outcome, and evidence limit. We do not publish a customer name, Logo, quotation, quantity, or result without permission.`

### What should I send for a reliable quote?

`Send the product category, reference images or tech pack, quantity or range, size range, fabric direction, artwork files, label and packaging needs, destination market, target timing, and any testing or compliance requirements. If a requirement is still unknown, mark it as open so it can be assigned and verified before approval.`

### Are the current website visuals factory photographs?

`Some current visuals are illustrative planning images. They are labeled accordingly and do not prove a named factory, staff roster, equipment, capacity, ownership, or completed customer order. Documentary media will be labeled with its date, scope, and authorization after verification.`

## 7. Safe structured-data mapping

After factual verification, map only public facts into JSON-LD:

- `Organization`: legal name, public URL, verified logo, public contact, and confirmed same-as profiles only.
- `ImageObject`: real image URL, accurate caption, date or content description where available, and no “factory” claim for illustrative media.
- `Certification` or document references: holder, issuer, identifier, scope, dates, and verification URL only when the page visibly explains the same information.
- `Review`: only a real, authorized review with reviewer identity or permitted anonymization; never generate a rating or quote.
- `CaseStudy` or `Article`: project facts, publication date, author/editorial policy, and disclosure of anonymization or limitations.

Do not add schema to imply a rating, certificate, factory ownership, employee count, production capacity, or customer relationship that the visible page cannot prove.
