# SEO Iteration — Sublimation Color Matching

Date: 2026-08-12

## Demand and intent

Current 2026 Reddit discussions repeatedly ask why a screen proof, color code, printed sheet, pressed sample, and finished textile do not look the same. Reddit is used only to document demand language. Technical statements are grounded in International Color Consortium device-profile guidance, Adobe color-proofing guidance, and the official ISO 105-J03 abstract.

The new article owns color target-to-output approval for sublimated sportswear. It does not compete with the commercial sublimated-teamwear page, the decoration-method page, the general artwork resource, or the whole-garment sample-to-bulk article.

## Evidence boundary

The iteration does not claim that a HEX, RGB, CMYK, named color code, screen mockup, ICC profile, photograph, instrument result, one physical specimen, or one universal Delta E limit guarantees an exact color match or every bulk unit. It does not diagnose Reddit examples, recommend one production setting, or assign responsibility for a dispute.

## Search Console boundary

Local and production validation can confirm crawlable HTML, self-canonical markup, sitemap membership, internal links, valid JSON-LD, downloadable assets, mobile layout, and HTTP delivery. It cannot prove Google indexing, Google-selected canonical, last crawl, sitemap processing, rankings, traffic, or AI-feature inclusion.

After deployment, inspect the new article and the updated custom-sublimated-teamwear, customization, and artwork URLs in the authenticated Search Console property. Record index status, user-declared and Google-selected canonical, last crawl, crawler type, fetch result, enhancements, sitemap processing, request-indexing action, and review date.
