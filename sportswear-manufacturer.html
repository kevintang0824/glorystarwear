Supplier comparison answers

OEM and ODM route questions

Compare the same scope, responsibilities, references, and evidence before treating two supplier proposals as equivalent.

What can a buyer send GloryStarWear for an OEM route review?

Send the current tech pack, measurements, material and trim specifications, artwork, labels, packaging, quantity and SKU structure, destination, required evidence, and open decisions. The response should identify feasibility conditions, proposed responsibilities, exclusions, and unresolved inputs.

What can a buyer send GloryStarWear for an ODM route review?

Send product references, target user and use, fit direction, material performance, branding, packaging, quantity structure, market position, destination, and target dates. Ask the response to separate proposed supplier development work from buyer decisions and approvals.

What should a GloryStarWear proposal identify before sampling?

Ask for the named contracting entity, proposed facility and partner roles, product scope, controlled inputs, development responsibility, material assumptions, sample stages, approval owners, quality evidence, packing, delivery handoff, exclusions, and open decisions.

Which records should identify the approved bulk-production reference?

Ask the proposal to name the approved sample ID and related file revisions, materials, colors, measurements, artwork, labels, packing, exceptions, and release decision that production and inspection would use.

What supplier evidence can a buyer request before comparing the proposal?

Request evidence relevant to the named entity, site, proposed route, material, sample workflow, certificate scope, inspection, packing, media availability, commercial assumptions, and subcontracted work. Check its source, date, relevance, scope, and limits before relying on it.

Ready to request a project-specific route review?

Send product references, quantity and SKU structure, logo files, target material, packing needs, destination, dates, and open decisions. Ask the written response to identify the proposed route, responsibilities, feasibility conditions, exclusions, and evidence still required.
