What does a one-stop sportswear service include?

A one-stop sportswear service connects product definition, material sourcing, sampling, branding, bulk production, inspection, retail packing, carton planning, and shipment handoff. It does not remove buyer approvals: each stage should name the required input, responsible party, deliverable, decision, and controlled reference before work moves forward.

Service questions

One-stop sportswear manufacturing FAQ

Is one-stop sportswear production the same as OEM or ODM?

No. One-stop describes the connected service scope. An OEM project follows buyer-controlled specifications, while an ODM project needs more supplier development input. Either route can include sampling, production, inspection, packaging, and shipment preparation.

Does one-stop service mean every task is performed by one factory?

Not necessarily. The responsible legal entity, operating location, subcontracted processes, material sources, quality ownership, and approval route should be disclosed and checked for the specific project. A single contact is not evidence that every operation is in-house.

When should labels and packaging be approved?

Approve label artwork, legal text, hangtags, barcodes, size stickers, folding, polybags, inserts, boxes, carton assortment, and shipping marks before bulk packing. Test scan-dependent or market-specific items at the appropriate sample stage.

What should a buyer send for a one-stop sportswear quote?

Send the product list, references or tech packs, quantity and SKU plan, target market, fabric and fit direction, artwork, labels, packaging, delivery country, timing, and the decisions that remain open.
