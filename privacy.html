Inquiry data

Privacy and Contact Information

This page explains what happens to inquiry information and how optional website analytics are controlled.

Current contact routes

How your inquiry is handled

GloryStarWear is the website-facing trade name. Verified legal-entity and registered-address details will be added when the supporting company records are approved for publication.

WhatsApp

When you choose WhatsApp, the site prepares your project brief and opens WhatsApp. The message is not sent until you confirm it in WhatsApp, whose own privacy terms then apply.

Email

When you choose email, the site prepares a message for your local email application. The website cannot confirm delivery; your email provider's terms apply after you send it.

Secure server form

This option uses Cloudflare Turnstile to reduce automated spam, then sends the completed brief to the same-origin Vercel endpoint. Vercel validates it and forwards it through an authenticated server-to-server connection to a dedicated Cloudflare D1 inquiry database. A successful submission is shown only after the database confirms storage, then the site opens the Thank You page.

Copy project brief

Copying uses your browser clipboard. The website does not treat a copied brief as a received inquiry.

Reference files

Files selected in the quote builder remain on your device unless you choose the device “Share Brief + Files” option and select a destination app. The secure server form records selected filenames only; it does not upload or store file contents. WhatsApp and email require you to attach files in those apps. Avoid personal information in filenames.

Data scope

Information used for your request

We ask for the minimum details needed to understand a sourcing project and reply through the contact route you choose.

Contact

Name, work email, and optional phone or WhatsApp

Used to identify and reply to the person making the request.

Project

Buyer type, route, product, quantity, market, timing, message, and selected filenames

Used to evaluate the request and prepare a relevant sourcing response. File contents are not sent through the secure form.

Source

Landing page, referring page, campaign tags, and source category

Stored in session storage and included in a prepared inquiry so the team can understand whether the conversation started from search, an AI assistant, social media, a campaign, another website, or a direct visit.

Optional analytics

Page visits, traffic source, and selected site actions

Google Analytics 4 loads only after you allow analytics. The site may then measure page views, quote starts, buyer-path choices, call requests, file-count and file-type categories, confirmed server submissions, contact clicks, resource downloads, product filters, and catalog searches. Analytics events do not include filenames, file contents, contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, or message text.

Protection

Do not send sensitive or confidential records initially

Avoid payment data, identity documents, passwords, or confidential tech packs until a suitable protected exchange route is agreed.

Your choices

Access, correction, retention, and deletion requests

Contact kevin@glorystarwears.com to ask about inquiry information, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable. Secure-form inquiry records are scheduled for deletion 365 days after receipt. A record may be removed sooner on a valid request or retained separately when reasonably required for an active quotation, ongoing business communication, security, dispute handling, or legal obligations.

Third parties

Vercel hosts the website and validates secure-form submissions. Cloudflare Workers and the dedicated Cloudflare D1 database receive and retain confirmed server-form inquiries. A destination app selected through your device share menu, WhatsApp, email providers, and—only after analytics permission—Google Analytics may process data when their service is used.

Analytics choice

The site stores granted or denied under glorystarwear-analytics-consent-v1 in browser local storage. When granted, Google Analytics may use first-party identifiers such as _ga. Advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization remain denied.

Updates

This notice will be updated when the legal entity, analytics platform, CRM, storage provider, retention period, or file-upload route changes.

Contact

Privacy and inquiry questions can be sent to the same business contact email shown on the website.
