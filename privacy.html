Inquiry data

Privacy and Contact Information

This page explains what happens to the information you enter when requesting a custom sportswear quote.

Current contact routes

How your inquiry is handled

GloryStarWear is the website-facing trade name. Verified legal-entity and registered-address details will be added when the supporting company records are approved for publication.

WhatsApp

When you choose WhatsApp, the site prepares your project brief and opens WhatsApp. The message is not sent until you confirm it in WhatsApp, whose own privacy terms then apply.

Email

When you choose email, the site prepares a message for your local email application. The website cannot confirm delivery; your email provider's terms apply after you send it.

Secure server form

This option is shown only when a real receiving service is configured. A successful submission means the receiving endpoint confirmed delivery and then opens the Thank You page.

Copy project brief

Copying uses your browser clipboard. The website does not treat a copied brief as a received inquiry.

Data scope

Information used for your request

We ask for the minimum details needed to understand a sourcing project and reply through the contact route you choose.

Contact

Name, work email, and optional phone or WhatsApp

Used to identify and reply to the person making the request.

Project

Product, quantity, market, timing, and message

Used to evaluate the request and prepare a relevant sourcing response.

Source

Landing page, referring page, campaign tags, and source category

Stored in session storage and included in a prepared inquiry so the team can understand whether the conversation started from search, an AI assistant, social media, a campaign, another website, or a direct visit.

Protection

Do not send sensitive or confidential records initially

Avoid payment data, identity documents, passwords, or confidential tech packs until a suitable protected exchange route is agreed.

Your choices

Access, correction, and deletion requests

Contact kevin@glorystarwears.com to ask about inquiry information, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable. Records may be retained when reasonably needed for a quotation, ongoing business communication, security, dispute handling, or legal obligations.

Third parties

WhatsApp, email providers, hosting services, and a configured lead-delivery provider may process data only when their service is used.

Analytics status

The site prepares vendor-neutral data-layer events, but no event should be treated as a received inquiry unless the server returns a successful response.

Updates

This notice will be updated when the legal entity, analytics platform, CRM, storage provider, retention period, or file-upload route changes.

Contact

Privacy and inquiry questions can be sent to the same business contact email shown on the website.
