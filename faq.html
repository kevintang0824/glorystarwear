Project routes

Can you support small test orders?

A project review can assess sample development or light-custom styles for a small test order. Exact MOQ, sample route, price, and scale-up conditions require confirmation for the submitted specification.

Do you support OEM and ODM sportswear?

A project review can assess OEM from a buyer-controlled tech pack and artwork, or ODM from references, target fabric, fit direction, market, and price range. The agreed route and responsibilities must be confirmed in writing.

What can be customized on ready styles?

Possible options include logo print, embroidery, woven labels, neck labels, hangtags, size labels, zipper pullers, colors, trims, and packaging. Availability and minimums depend on the selected base style and project.

Can you make full private-label designs?

A private-label project review can cover patterns, fabrics, trims, artwork placement, sample revisions, proposed production, inspection, labels, and packaging. Feasibility and responsibility are confirmed after the brief is reviewed.

What products can GloryStarWear produce?

The published product planning range covers yoga wear, athleisure, men's and women's training wear, basketball wear, football kits, sports accessories, and branded packaging. Exact feasibility is confirmed per specification.

What should I send for a fast quote?

Send reference photos, logo files, order quantity, size range, fabric target, target market, packaging needs, and expected delivery time.

Do you provide packaging?

A packaging review can cover polybags, boxes, tissue, cards, stickers, hangtags, barcode labels, care labels, size labels, and carton marks. Exact materials, labeling responsibility, minimums, and packing route require project confirmation.

Can you make team orders?

A teamwear project review can cover club colors, sponsor logos, names, numbers, roster packing, size sorting, and reorder records. Sport rules, quantities, sizes, and deadlines must be confirmed for the named program.

How do you control quality before shipment?

A project-specific quality plan can define fabric, color, fit, measurement, stitching, logo placement, labels, packing, carton marks, sampling, inspection, and release evidence. The agreed checks and acceptance rules belong in the quotation and approval record.

Can you help choose sportswear fabric?

Material selection can compare nylon spandex, polyester spandex, fleece, French terry, mesh, rib, seamless knit, or recycled-fabric directions against the product goal. Final composition, color, performance claims, testing, and availability require coded-swatch and project review.
