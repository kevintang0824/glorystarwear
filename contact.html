Commercial boundaries

What we confirm before quoting and production

MOQ, sample cost, lead time, reorder terms, and production scope are confirmed against the exact product—not presented as universal promises.

MOQ basis

Style, color, fabric, trim, and decoration drive the minimum

We confirm whether the minimum applies per style, color, artwork, or total program after reviewing the brief.

Sample stage

Purpose, fee, revisions, and courier are listed separately

Prototype, fit, size-set, sales, pre-production, and shipment samples may have different requirements.

Timing clock

Lead time starts after defined approvals and inputs

Material booking, artwork, payment, approved sample, size breakdown, and packaging files can affect the start date.

Reorders

Availability and consistency are rechecked before repeat production

Fabric lots, colors, trims, labels, capacity, and approved specifications are reviewed rather than assumed unchanged.

Production scope

Included and buyer-supplied responsibilities are named

Tech pack, pattern, sourcing, printing, labels, packing, inspection, freight, testing, and import duties are separated in writing.

Response route

Email and WhatsApp are available; calls can be requested in the form

The response window is confirmed with the project owner after receipt instead of showing an unverified service-time guarantee.
