Each example below is a planning prompt. It contains no actual buyer, order, production, delivery, reorder, testimonial, or outcome fact. A verified project story may replace a prompt only after the buyer approves the public facts, media, metrics, and publication scope.

Illustrative brief: Private label yoga capsule

Define proposed leggings, sports bras, biker shorts, hangtags, size labels, polybags, and a coordinated color direction for a first-launch quote.

Illustrative brief: Gym merchandise range

Compare a proposed light-custom route for tees, tanks, shorts, compression tops, towels, socks, and logo placement.

Illustrative brief: Club teamwear program

Prepare a proposed roster for football kits, basketball uniforms, names, numbers, sponsor artwork, player packing, and repeat-order SKU control.

Illustrative brief: Packaging upgrade

Specify proposed woven labels, hangtags, barcode labels, polybags, carton marks, and SKU sorting for an ecommerce or wholesale packing quote.
