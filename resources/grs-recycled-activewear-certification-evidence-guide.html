Recycled-material verification

Understanding GRS-Certified Recycled Activewear in 2026

Verify the organization, site, product scope, order transaction, chain of custody, certified-content basis, and claim approval before treating a recycled activewear order as certified.

Download Evidence RegisterCheck Certificate EvidenceDiscuss a Material Brief

Direct answer

What evidence should a buyer request for GRS activewear?

Request a valid scope certificate for the exact certified organization and site, confirm that the relevant product and process appear within its scope, then obtain the transaction certificate covering the actual certified goods and shipment. Reconcile seller, buyer, material, certified content, quantities, invoice or shipment references, dates, and any processors. Separately confirm whether the intended product claim or logo is permitted and approved. A supplier scope certificate alone does not certify a specific purchase order.

By GloryStarWear product development teamPublished and updated August 29, 2026Rules and transition dates checked against official sourcesOpen source list

Scope certificate

Shows that a named organization and site are eligible for listed products and processes during the stated validity period. It is a capability and scope record, not proof of your shipment.

Transaction certificate

Connects certified products to a specific transaction or shipment, including the seller, buyer, goods, quantities, and references that the buyer must reconcile.

Claim approval

Product wording and logo use follow separate certification and artwork-approval rules. Receiving certified material or a transaction certificate does not automatically grant public claim rights.

Change control

Recheck evidence when the material, supplier, certified site, processor, factory, finish, component, order, shipment, or intended claim changes.

Evidence ladder

Do not let one document stand in for the whole chain

Use each record for the question it can actually answer. Names, addresses, identifiers, dates, products and quantities should agree across the controlled order package.

Important boundary

This page does not claim that GloryStarWear is GRS certified

This is a buyer verification guide. It does not identify GloryStarWear, a factory, a fabric mill, a product, or a customer order as certified. Any project-specific statement requires the current named certificate holder, site, scope, validity, authenticated transaction evidence, and claim route.

Scope versus transaction

Reconcile the actual activewear order

  1. Name the intended claim

    Write whether the need is internal sourcing evidence, a business-to-business statement, a consumer product-specific claim, or logo use. Do not begin with a vague request for a “GRS certificate.”

  2. Identify every legal party and site

    Record the material supplier, seller, garment manufacturer, processors, brand, buyer and destination. Match names and addresses rather than relying on trading names.

  3. Authenticate the current scope

    Check the certificate status, holder, site, standard, dates, products and processes through Textile Exchange authentication and the named certification body.

  4. Match the ordered product

    Connect style, component, material code, composition, certified-content basis, color or finish, supplier, process and production site to the approved BOM and PO.

  5. Request the transaction record

    Confirm that the transaction certificate covers the actual seller-to-buyer transaction and reconciles with invoice, shipment, quantities and certified goods.

  6. Close every chain gap

    Escalate a different seller, site, processor, material, percentage, quantity, reference or expired document. Record the explanation and supporting evidence instead of inferring coverage.

  7. Approve claims separately

    Have the responsible certified organization and certification body check eligibility, exact wording, artwork, market use, approval reference and duration before publication or printing.

  8. Repeat for changes and reorders

    Review the current chain again when an order, shipment, supplier, material, facility, processor, finish, packaging claim or rule version changes.

Different questions

GRS and OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 are not substitutes

ProgramCore evidence questionBuyer must still verify
GRSRecycled material content and chain-of-custody certification within the listed scope and transaction.Exact holder, site, product, process, current version, order transaction, content basis and claim approval.
OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100Whether the tested textile article and components meet the program's harmful-substance requirements for the stated certificate scope.Certificate authenticity, holder, product class, article coverage, validity and exact product relevance.

One can be relevant alongside the other, but neither should be presented as evidence for the question answered by the other. Also do not infer garment performance, durability, labor conditions, origin or a complete market-access conclusion from either label alone.

FAQ

GRS recycled activewear evidence questions

Does a supplier scope certificate prove my activewear order is GRS certified?

No. A valid scope certificate shows that the named organization and site are eligible for listed products and processes within its scope. The buyer must still verify the actual order and shipment through the relevant transaction certificate and reconcile the goods, parties, quantities, dates and references.

What does a GRS transaction certificate prove?

It verifies certified products in a specific transaction or shipment and identifies details such as the seller, buyer, goods and shipment references. It should be authenticated and matched to the PO, invoice, packing and material records; it does not by itself authorize every public claim or logo use.

Can a brand use the GRS logo after receiving certified material?

Not automatically. Textile Exchange explains that the chain must meet the applicable certification rules through the relevant seller, and product-specific claims or standard-logo artwork follow an approval route with the certification body. Confirm the exact organization, claim type, wording, artwork and market use before publication.

What is the difference between GRS and OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100?

GRS addresses recycled material content and chain of custody within a certified scope and transaction. OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 addresses harmful-substance testing for the certified textile article and components. They answer different evidence questions and neither should be used as proof of the other's scope.

What changes require recycled-material evidence to be checked again?

Recheck when the material, supplier, certified organization, site, processor, garment factory, content basis, color or finish, component, order, shipment, quantity, seller, buyer, public claim, packaging artwork, certificate validity or applicable rule changes.

Does this page show that GloryStarWear is GRS certified?

No. This page is a buyer verification guide and makes no project-specific certification claim for GloryStarWear, a factory, a material, a product or a customer order. Such a claim requires current authenticated evidence identifying the exact holder, site, scope, validity, transaction and claim route.

Send the style, material target, claim intent and evidence you already have.
